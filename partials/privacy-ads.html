Advertising

We advertise on Google. To see which ads bring people to the site, and to show our ads to people who have visited before, our pages load the Google Ads tag. It uses cookies to record your visit, which pages you view, and whether you send a quote request or place an order. Google can combine this with what it already knows about your Google account to decide which ads to show you, on Google and on other sites.

This counts as targeted advertising. You can opt out of it:

  • Turn off personalized ads in Google's ad settings.
  • Opt out of interest-based ads from many companies at once through the Digital Advertising Alliance or the Network Advertising Initiative.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser.
  • Email us at {{email}} to ask us not to use your information for targeted advertising.

Opting out doesn't stop ads. It means the ads you see aren't based on your visit here.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including Texas, you may have the right to ask what personal information we have about you, to correct or delete it, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Email {{email}} and we'll respond within 45 days. If we turn down a request, you can reply to appeal and we'll look at it again.
