Privacy

Tyler Manufacturing, LLC runs this website. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

Quote requests. What you type into the quote form. The form is handled by Web3Forms, which emails it to us.

How the site is used. We use Google Analytics to see which pages people visit and how they found us. It uses cookies to collect things like the pages you view, your device and browser, roughly where you are (city or region), and the site that sent you here. Google processes this for us under its privacy policy. To opt out, block cookies in your browser or install Google's opt-out add-on.

Emails you send us, including any drawings.

How we use it

To answer your request, quote your job, and make and ship your order.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and we don't use advertising trackers on this site.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your browser's IP address when you visit.
